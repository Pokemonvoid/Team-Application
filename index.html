What happens after you apply

  1. Submit your application

    Choose one or more open roles and complete the questions relevant to you.

  2. Director review

    Your application enters the private Director queue for review.

  3. Application update

    Important updates can be sent to your Discord account so you do not have to keep refreshing the site.

  4. Interview or onboarding

    If you progress, the Directors will continue the process with you directly. Some roles may move to Discord for a live interview.

Before you apply

  • Applicants of any age may apply.
  • Applicants under 18 require guardian permission, a portfolio, and a referral.
  • You may apply for more than one open role.
  • Your Discord account will identify your recruitment profile, so you will not need a separate username and password.
  • Have examples of your work ready where the role asks for them.

What happens after you apply

  1. Submit your applicationSign in with Discord and complete the questions for every role you select.
  2. Director reviewYour application enters the private recruitment queue. Updates can be sent to you through Discord.
  3. Application ticketIf the Directors want to continue, questions and replies can be handled through your private recruitment ticket.
  4. Live follow-up if neededSome applicants may be invited to a secluded area of the development server for a live interview or additional questions.
  5. OnboardingSuccessful applicants are welcomed into the development team and introduced to the people they will work with.

Before you start

Any age can apply. Be honest about your experience and availability. You may apply for more than one open role in the same application.

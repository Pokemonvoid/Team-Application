How recruitment works

  1. Open the recruitment portal

    Follow the recruitment link from the Pokémon Void Discord server and sign in with Discord.

  2. Complete your application

    Choose one or more open roles and answer the general and role-specific questions.

  3. Watch for an update

    Application updates can be sent to you through Discord while the Directors review your submission.

  4. Continue to interview

    If you progress, the Directors will continue with a private interview ticket. Some applicants may later be invited to a secluded Discord area for live questions.

Before you apply

  • You may apply for more than one open role.
  • Applicants must be at least 16 years old.
  • Applicants aged 16–17 require guardian permission, a portfolio, and a referral.
  • You can return to your profile to check your current application status.

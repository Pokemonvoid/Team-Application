HOW IT WORKS

A straightforward application process.

  1. 01

    Connect Discord

    Use Discord's own authorization screen. The portal never asks you for your Discord password or 2FA details.

  2. 02

    Complete your application

    Select a development role, answer the required questions, and provide portfolio links or supporting material where relevant.

  3. 03

    Director review

    Your submission enters a private recruitment queue. Important status changes can later be mirrored through a lightweight Discord notification bot.

  4. 04

    Interview, if selected

    If your application progresses, you receive a private asynchronous recruitment ticket. You do not need to join another Discord server.

Recruitment Help

Quick answers about applying, interviews, Discord, and portfolios.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a separate account for this site?

No. The live portal will use Discord authorization. You sign in through Discord and the recruitment profile is linked to that Discord account.

Can I apply for more than one role?

Yes. Choose every currently open role you genuinely want to apply for. The form will show the relevant questions for each selection.

Which roles are open?

Programming, Move Animation, Spriting, and Music are included in the current application flow. Other roles are temporarily closed.

How old do I need to be?

Applicants must be at least 16. Applicants aged 16–17 require legal guardian permission, a portfolio, and a referral. Applicants aged 18 or older do not need guardian permission.

Where will application updates appear?

The portal will show your current status. Important updates can also be sent to your Discord account so you do not need to repeatedly check the site.

How do interviews work?

Applicants who progress can receive a private interview ticket. Director identities may remain hidden during this stage. Some roles or final-stage applicants may be invited to a secluded area of the development Discord for live questions and onboarding.

What about large programmer video submissions?

Do not worry about finding a permanent host just for the application. If a programming review needs a large direct video submission, the Directors can contact you and arrange a suitable transfer method.

Does the site ever ask for my Discord password?

No. Discord credentials must only be entered on Discord's own authorization page. This portal only receives the account information Discord authorizes it to use.

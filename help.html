RECRUITMENT HELP

Questions before applying?

Basic applicant guidance. Final recruitment policy wording can be replaced without changing the site structure.

Why do I need to connect Discord?

Discord identifies your recruitment profile, helps prevent duplicate applications, and gives the recruitment team a reliable way to associate your submission with your server account.

Does Pokémon Void receive my Discord password?

No. Authentication will happen through Discord OAuth on Discord's own authorization page. The portal should never receive your password, passkey or 2FA code.

Do I need to keep checking another website?

The portal is where your application and ticket live, but the planned tertiary Discord bot can send important notifications so applicants do not have to repeatedly check for changes.

Where will interviews happen?

Applicants selected for interview will receive access to a private asynchronous recruitment ticket. The final encrypted interview implementation is being designed separately from the public application form.

Can I upload portfolio files?

The final upload rules depend on storage limits. Small image files may be supported directly. Large videos should generally use links or be arranged with recruitment when a role genuinely requires them.

What happens if my application is declined?

The Directors will decide the final notification and retention policy. The portal supports keeping declined applications out of the active queue without exposing them to unrelated staff.
